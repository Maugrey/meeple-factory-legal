Privacy integration contracts

An orchestration contract for integrations: it does not mandate a vendor API and must be confirmed after the SDKs are frozen.

Age state

Only a persisted minimum-age category is allowed. Date of birth and exact age are never retained. Until age is known, the profile is treated as protected.

Order and independence

First launch: 1) age; 2) UGS Analytics choice; 3) UMP only when required. Age state, UGS choice, UMP state and minimal Aptabase are independent: none infers or replaces another.

Reconfiguration and settings

An age correction or consent change reassesses and reconfigures affected adapters. Settings expose age, UGS, UMP, deletion and legal links. Adapters do not expose their own gameplay UI.

Advertising and offline

Advertising remains unavailable until age is resolved and, where applicable, UMP is resolved. Offline, external operations may be deferred or unavailable; the protective posture remains.

Responsibilities

UGS/UMP adapters apply their state and report availability; Aptabase remains minimal and separate. The orchestrator coordinates order, persists allowed choices, triggers reconfiguration and supplies state to settings. Final compliance, inventories, retention and store disclosures remain to be confirmed after the SDKs are frozen.
