Privacy policy

Revision 1.3 — published and effective: 30 October 2026.

This policy describes the implemented game flow. Provider retention and transfer details below distinguish public provider information from settings that have not been independently verified.

Publisher and scope

Counting Twice is the public studio name for Meeple Factory. The publisher operates as an individual; Counting Twice is not represented as an incorporated company. This policy covers the Android game Meeple Factory and this information site. For questions about this policy or your data, write to countingtwice@maugrey.net or see our support page.

On your device

The game saves progression, virtual resources, settings and a declared age group, including the threshold used. No birth date or exact age is requested or saved. Choices are: under the local threshold, threshold–17, 18–25, 26–40 and 41 and over. The mobile network or SIM country determines the threshold: 15 in France, the configured territorial rule elsewhere, and a protective fallback of 16 for missing, conflicting or unrecognized signals. No country code is saved and no location permission is requested. UGS Analytics stays disabled below the threshold; other players can accept or refuse it separately. Missing, invalid or legacy age data grants no authorization. Correcting the group preserves progression.

The accepted terms revision and UTC acceptance date are stored locally, independently of age. UGS choices, setup progress and deletion requests are saved separately. Google UMP stores its own advertising choices. There is no global acceptance: terms or legal-page access never grant Analytics or advertising consent. There is no player account; the country signal only selects the protective threshold. The category does not change difficulty. Uninstalling removes the app-managed local data; an operating-system backup may restore it. Uninstalling does not automatically delete data already received by providers.

Optional local reminders

If you enable reminders, the game stores your notification preferences and the local scheduling information needed to show them. Reminders are scheduled on your device, without a remote push-notification service. You can change the categories in the game settings or disable notifications in Android settings. Notification permission is separate from statistics and advertising choices.

Aptabase: minimal measurement for everyone

Aptabase operates independently of UGS, before its choice, after refusal or withdrawal and for protected profiles. Six event families are allowed: session_started (first session, reason, build channel), analytics_choice_recorded (grant/refusal when offered), ftue_milestone (step, order, channel), round_summary (outcome, broad level/duration/score groups, continuation, replay, channel), challenge_progress (victory, bundle awarded, channel), and ad_result (fixed ad placement, format, result, reward, latency group, channel). No per-tap events, exact balances, ad revenue, age, category, shared UUID or transaction identifier are added.

The SDK attaches its timestamp, technical session, app version/build, OS/version, locale and debug status. In the shipped Unity 0.3.1 SDK, sdkVersion identifies Aptabase.Unity; the event body has no separate device-model or engine-version field. Client characteristics may also appear in the network User-Agent; this is distinct from a durable identifier.

At ingestion, Technov Solutions SRL uses IP and User-Agent temporarily in memory to derive country/region and a daily pseudonymous identifier. Country/region is stored with analytics; raw IP and User-Agent are not retained. The app-specific salt rotates every 24 hours and is permanently deleted within 48 hours of creation. These measurements do not track someone across days or apps. The selected region is the EU. The provider states a maximum of five years for analytics data; that does not prove the selected account’s period. See Aptabase privacy and its processing agreement. Stored data normally cannot identify a player for individual deletion; the provider can assist when identification is possible.

UGS Analytics: your separate permission

Protected profiles are never asked and send nothing to UGS Analytics. For eligible profiles, collection defaults to denied. After permission, Unity receives the game’s sessions, tutorial milestones, round starts/outcomes, progression, actions, virtual-currency transactions and advertising transitions. These contain game session/event/transaction identifiers, times, language, level, durations, scores, quantities and states needed for those analyses. Unity can also process IP, installation/user identifiers and its SDK’s technical events. This flow does not use Unity Authentication accounts.

Initial consent starts first-session measurement. Later consent sends only the approved current snapshot and future events, with no historical replay. The declared age group accompanies authorized UGS events to compare groups of players. Birth date and exact age are never collected. The purpose is to understand the player journey and improve the game, based on consent that can be withdrawn without changing gameplay. Unity acts as an Analytics processor. Authorized publisher personnel and the service’s technical providers can access the data.

Unity describes servers in Belgium, Snowflake storage in the Netherlands and a copy in Iowa. Its documentation distinguishes 13-month raw-event retention from metrics/profiles held until deletion. Those public statements are not evidence of this project’s configuration. See the Analytics FAQ and privacy overview.

Google UMP and AdMob

Google UMP refreshes choices at each launch and shows only necessary forms. UGS consent never authorizes ads. Refusal and limited choices remain resolved; a new provider requirement may require a form at launch. AdMob loads no ad without current UMP authorization. On technical failure, ads remain unavailable and gameplay is accessible after the local setup steps.

The game offers rewarded and interstitial ads under its advertising policy. Protected profiles receive Families treatment, without personalization, and Google’s required technical protection signals; Google receives child treatment below the threshold, teen treatment through 17, or standard treatment from 18, without the statistical adult age group. Child and teen treatments disable personalization. Google may process IP/inferred approximate location, interactions and ad views, diagnostics/performance and identifiers allowed by configuration and choices. Purposes include delivering/measuring ads and preventing fraud. Consent-dependent processing follows UMP decisions; necessary security processing follows Google’s stated purposes and legal bases. This flow requests no location permission.

Recipients include Google and the advertising providers identified in the applicable form. Transfers and retention vary by processing and are not established by a local game timeout. See the SDK disclosure, Google privacy policy, retention rules and transfer safeguards. This game version implements no purchases, payments or refunds.

For Google’s processing, also see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Bases, withdrawal and rights

Local progression and settings provide the requested game functionality; protection and choice records support applicable obligations. Minimal usage measurement serves the legitimate interest of understanding game operation, without durable tracking or advertising targeting from those measurements. Anonymous aggregates are not individual records. The technical processing that produces them, including IP, remains described above; lack of a durable identifier does not eliminate that stage. Detailed UGS collection and optional advertising processing use their separate consents.

At startup, choose your age group, then accept the terms of use in “Before you start”. The optional “I allow detailed usage statistics to be sent to improve the game” checkbox is off for a new eligible player and absent below the protected threshold. Legal information and links remain available to everyone. Continue depends only on the terms; accepting them or opening a link never grants statistics or advertising permission. Existing profiles retain their age group and statistics choice and are asked once to accept the terms revision.

Settings → Privacy combines the editable age group, detailed statistics checkbox, deletion request, advertising choices when available and legal links. Switching off stops UGS immediately and purges local queues without requesting erasure. Switching on permits the approved current snapshot and future events without replaying history. Moving below the threshold stops collection; moving above it leaves collection off until a new agreement. Changes save immediately without accepting the terms again.

Deletion requires confirmation, stops collection and preserves game progress. Offline requests remain saved and resume after reconnection or restart. Collection cannot be enabled while a request is pending. “Request saved” means locally pending; “Request sent” means upload acknowledgment and does not prove completed provider erasure. The link remains available below the protected threshold for any earlier data. Minimal Aptabase remains independent.

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and, where applicable, portability, or withdraw consent through the existing public contact described on the support page. Rights depend on data that can actually be identified; no new tracking identity is created to find anonymous data. For Google-managed data, also use its privacy controls and request channels. You may complain to the French CNIL. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.
