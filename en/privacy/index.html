Privacy policy

Data inventories, recipients, retention periods and transfers remain provisional until the SDKs are frozen. Such points are explicitly marked for confirmation.

Current principles

The app is designed to retain only a minimum age category, never a date of birth or exact age. Protected profiles receive the most protective treatment by default.

Choices and optional services

At first launch, the app asks for age, then the UGS Analytics choice; UMP is displayed only when required. Those choices are independent. If Aptabase is used, it is limited to minimal measurement and remains independent.

Data and purposes

To confirm before release: exact shipped SDKs, data categories, purposes, legal bases, retention periods, possible transfers and store disclosures.

Your settings

The app must allow correction of the age category, changes to UGS/UMP choices when available, data-deletion requests, and legal links. Correcting age or changing a choice reconfigures the affected services.

Offline

When offline, external links and network requests may be unavailable. The app must retain a protective behaviour and must not make ads available before age and, where required, UMP have been resolved.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning your data, see the support page. The definitive contact address remains to be confirmed before release.
