Privacy policy

1. Scope and controller

This policy covers the legal information website and the intended behaviour of Meeple Factory v1.0 on Android / Google Play. The game does not plan to offer player accounts or progress synchronisation between devices. Planned features still need to be verified in the distributed build.

To confirm before distribution: the legal identity, address and contact details of the publisher responsible for processing. The game name and GitHub account do not replace this information. A private contact channel will be published on the support page.

2. When you visit this website

The publisher adds no trackers, advertising, application cookies, forms or accounts. The host, GitHub Pages, logs and retains visitors’ IP addresses for security, even without a signed-in GitHub account. GitHub manages these logs; its policy governs their retention, recipients and transfers.

See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement. External links open services governed by their own rules; no third-party measurement resources are embedded in these pages.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves progress, virtual resources and items, rewards, preferences and information needed to resume play locally. Version 1.0 must also remember the minimum age category and privacy choices needed to apply them.

A date or age entered to determine protection is processed temporarily on the device: neither date of birth nor exact age is retained. The category stays local; it is sent to neither Aptabase nor Unity Gaming Services Analytics. Only the necessary protection flags are passed to the advertising service.

4. Minimal measurement with Aptabase

The project plans to use Aptabase Cloud in the European Union region for all profiles, including before a UGS choice, after refusal or withdrawal, and for protected profiles. This measurement helps understand overall game usage; the “Detailed UGS Analytics” control does not turn it off.

The baseline is limited to five families: session starts, UGS choices actually offered, tutorial milestones, round summaries and advertising outcomes. Values such as duration or score are grouped into ranges. No persistent player identifier, transaction identifier, age, age category, free text or advertising identifier is added to these events. No shared identifier allows them to be linked to UGS data.

The SDK also sends a technical session and app version/build, operating system, language/locale, device model, engine/SDK and development-mode information. The service processes the IP address and User-Agent to calculate a daily technical identity, without persistent tracking of a player across days. “Minimal” therefore does not mean that no technical data is processed.

To confirm before distribution: the exact SDK inventory, EU configuration and legal justification for operation without consent, especially for children. Aggregation and the absence of a persistent identifier do not, by themselves, establish an exemption. See the CNIL audience-measurement conditions (French).

5. Detailed measurement with Unity Gaming Services Analytics

UGS Analytics is planned only for non-protected profiles that explicitly accept it. It helps analyse journeys, progression, the virtual economy, advertising and returning players. It uses a pseudonymous installation identifier and detailed events, with round or transaction identifiers needed to reconcile them. This data is pseudonymous, not anonymous.

Without consent, after refusal or withdrawal, or for a protected profile, no UGS event is sent or retained for later delivery. Late consent starts measurement at that moment, with the intended current state and no replay of history. Withdrawal stops collection and clears the unsent queue; it does not automatically delete data already received by Unity.

The game adds no name, email address, free text, precise location, advertising identifier, age or age category to telemetry. To confirm: automatic metadata, retention, recipients and transfers of the shipped SDK. See the Unity player privacy policy.

6. Google AdMob advertising and UMP choices

Version 1.0 plans to use Google AdMob directly, with rewarded and interstitial ads and no advertising mediation. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) determines which advertising choices to present and keeps the information needed to apply them. UMP consent never gives UGS consent, or vice versa.

The provisional AdMob inventory includes IP address (which may be used to estimate a general location), advertising interactions, diagnostics and, depending on settings and profile, device or advertising identifiers. Purposes include delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Non-personalised ads may still require technical processing.

Protected profiles must receive only child-appropriate ads under Google Play Families rules, without personalisation or remarketing. No ad is loaded before completion of the age flow and UMP authorisation to request ads. A limited choice does not guarantee ad availability.

To confirm: AdMob and UMP data actually processed by the shipped versions, legal bases per purpose, recipients, retention and transfers. See Google’s SDK data information and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children and first launch

The game is intended for children and adults. A neutral age screen is planned before age-dependent services, with no preselected adult answer or skip option. An interruption brings this screen back. A missing, invalid or unknown category receives protected treatment.

After age, the UGS choice is offered only to eligible profiles, with refusal as accessible as acceptance; UMP follows if required. Protected profiles are not asked for UGS consent. Applicable thresholds for distribution territories remain to be confirmed. The category is used neither for analytics segmentation nor to adjust difficulty.

8. Retention and transfers

  • Local data: kept in application data until erased or replaced. Clearing app data or uninstalling may cause progress loss. Android backup/restore behaviour depends on the final configuration and still needs verification.
  • Aptabase: its policy consulted on 7 September 2026 states retention of analytics data for up to five years. This provider maximum is not a duration selected and validated for Meeple Factory; the chosen period, options to shorten it and the EU region must be documented before distribution.
  • UGS, AdMob and UMP: retention of events, identifiers, choices, queues and provider backups must be confirmed separately; no immediate universal deletion is promised.
  • Support: categories and retention of correspondence will be defined with the official channel.

Aptabase is planned for the EU region. This does not guarantee that every other service processes data exclusively within the EU. Contracting entities, subprocessors, processing locations, possible transfers outside the European Economic Area and relevant safeguards remain to be confirmed for each provider.

9. Your choices and rights

Planned privacy settings allow age correction, accepting or stopping UGS, requesting UGS deletion, opening advertising choices when UMP requires them, and reading these pages. Refusing or withdrawing UGS does not block the game or change advertising eligibility.

Stopping, deleting and uninstalling are different. A UGS deletion request must first stop collection and then use the dedicated deletion process. Offline, it remains pending and must resume after reconnection or restart; it must not be shown as complete before confirmation. Uninstalling the game does not send a deletion request to Unity or Google.

Aptabase states that it cannot find one person’s events for individual deletion. We therefore do not promise deletion of these statistics by identifying a person; applicable arrangements remain to be confirmed. No additional identifier will be created solely to associate them with a player.

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability, and withdraw consent for future processing. A parent or representative may contact support for a child. The private channel needed for these requests still has to be published. You may also contact the competent authority, including the CNIL in France (French).

10. Finalisation items

Publisher identity and contact, legal bases per purpose, age thresholds, actual inventories, retention, transfers and rights procedures must be completed before distribution beyond internal testing. The finalisation register lists the required evidence. This draft does not validate the game’s compliance.
