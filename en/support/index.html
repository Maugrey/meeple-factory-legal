Support and contact

Draft v0.2 — published: 7 September 2026
Effective date of the final documents: to confirm before distribution.

Official channel in preparation

No support or privacy contact address has been published yet. The publisher’s legal name and a working private channel will be added here before game distribution beyond internal testing. This page is not a form and cannot send a request.

Do not post personal data, saves or game logs in the public repository’s issues. The repository hosts documents; it is not a channel for individual privacy requests.

Preparing a bug report

Once contact is available, include the game version, Android version, device model, steps leading to the issue and expected result. For an advertising reward, explain the context and whether the game was interrupted. Hide personal information in screenshots and share logs only if support requests them through an appropriate channel.

Never share passwords, banking data or dates of birth. This page does not request a copy of an identity document.

Choices and data deletion

In the planned v1.0, “Privacy” settings must offer age correction, the UGS choice, UGS deletion and available advertising choices. These features still need validation in the released version. Stopping UGS does not delete data already sent; uninstalling does not delete it at providers either.

A UGS request made offline must remain pending until transmission and confirmation. If you have already uninstalled the game, options to locate the data must be examined through the future private contact; identity recovery is not guaranteed. Aptabase does not offer a person-based search of statistics. See the privacy policy.

Parents and representatives

The future private channel will accept requests concerning a child. Provide only the information needed for the request; do not publish the child’s name or age in the public repository.

Offline use and local progress

An unavailable link or advertising service must not prevent play. Clearing application data may cause progress loss; this is separate from a request to delete remote data.

To complete before distribution

Publisher identity, email for private contact, handling of requests and representatives, proportionate verification where needed, applicable time limits and retention of correspondence. No commercial response-time commitment is made at this stage.
