Support and contact

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

Get help

To contact the individual publisher using the public name Counting Twice, write to countingtwice@maugrey.net. You can use this address even if you do not have access to the Google Play test. This page contains no submission form. Do not post personal information in the public document repository.

A game problem

Describe the issue, app version, device operating system and steps to reproduce it. Do not send your birth date, passwords, advertising identifiers, payment information or a complete save. An optional screenshot can help if it contains no private information.

If an ad or link fails, check the connection and retry. Gameplay can continue without available ads. Advertising rewards require the game’s qualifying result. Privacy choices remain independent of rewards and progression.

Privacy and deletion

Settings → Privacy lets you correct the age category, stop UGS Analytics and explicitly request its data deletion. An offline request resumes after reconnection or restart; keep the app installed until upload. An upload receipt does not prove completed server deletion. For access, objection or deletion follow-up, use the existing public contact described above and identify the service concerned without providing your birth date. See the policy for Aptabase’s anonymous-data limitations and Google controls.

The terms are available here. This site manages no accounts, purchases or subscriptions.

Parents and representatives

The same contact accepts requests concerning a child. Only provide the information necessary to handle the request.
